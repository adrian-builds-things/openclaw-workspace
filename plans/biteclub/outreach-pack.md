# Bite Club – Outreach Pack (LinkedIn + Email + Follow-ups)

_Last updated: 2026-02-16_

Use these as **copy/paste templates**. Personalize line 1 + pain point before sending.

## 1) Target persona shortcuts
- Office/Workplace Manager
- People Ops / HR Ops Lead
- COO / Head of Operations

## 2) Personalization variables
- `{first_name}`
- `{company}`
- `{role}`
- `{context_trigger}` (office growth, RTO, hiring, cost pressure, etc.)
- `{social_proof_or_relevance}`
- `{cta_link}`

---

## 3) LinkedIn Connection Request (short)

**Template A**
Hi {first_name}, saw you’re driving workplace operations at {company}. We help teams simplify office lunch ops and reduce admin overhead. Thought it could be relevant to connect.

**Template B**
Hi {first_name}, quick one: I’m speaking with ops leaders on reducing lunch/catering chaos in hybrid offices. Your work at {company} looked relevant — happy to connect.

---

## 4) LinkedIn DM – First Message (after accept)

**Template A (problem-led)**
Thanks for connecting, {first_name}. Quick question: how are you currently managing office lunch coordination at {company}?

We’re helping ops teams reduce manual coordination and improve cost visibility without adding process complexity.

If useful, I can send a short 2-minute breakdown with benchmarks we’re seeing.

**Template B (trigger-led)**
Thanks for connecting, {first_name}. Noticed {context_trigger} at {company}.

Usually this is when lunch ops start becoming messy (manual vendor coordination, low visibility, waste). We built Bite Club to fix exactly that for office teams.

Open to a short exchange if this is on your radar?

---

## 5) LinkedIn Follow-up Sequence

## Follow-up 1 (2–3 days later)
Wanted to bubble this up, {first_name} — should I send over a concise lunch-ops checklist our customers use before evaluating tooling?

## Follow-up 2 (4–5 days later)
If this isn’t a priority now, no stress. I can also circle back in a few weeks.

Before I do: are lunch ops currently handled centrally or by each office/team separately?

## Follow-up 3 (breakup, 7+ days)
Last nudge from my side. If improving lunch ops (less admin + better cost control) becomes relevant later, happy to reconnect.

---

## 6) Cold Email Templates

## Email 1 – Problem + curiosity
**Subject options:**
- Quick question on lunch ops at {company}
- Reducing lunch admin for office teams
- Is this a pain at {company}?

Hi {first_name},

I’m reaching out because many {role}-teams we speak with run into similar lunch challenges:
- too much manual coordination,
- unclear spend/usage visibility,
- and inconsistent employee experience across office days.

We built Bite Club to make office lunch operations simpler and measurable.

Would it be unreasonable to compare your current setup to what high-performing teams are doing in a 15-minute call?

Best,
{sender_name}

## Email 2 – Value + soft CTA (if no reply, +3 days)
**Subject:** Re: lunch ops at {company}

Hi {first_name},

Sharing this in case useful: teams evaluating Bite Club usually want three outcomes first:
1) less admin workload,
2) better budget transparency,
3) smoother lunch experience for employees.

If this is relevant, I can send a one-page framework to assess whether changing your setup is worth it.

Open to that?

Best,
{sender_name}

## Email 3 – Breakup (after +5–7 days)
**Subject:** close this out?

Hi {first_name},

I’ll close the loop for now.

If lunch operations/catering efficiency becomes a priority, I’m happy to share benchmarks and a practical rollout path.

Either way, thanks for reading.

Best,
{sender_name}

---

## 7) Objection handling snippets (quick replies)

**"No budget"**
Understood. In similar cases we start with a narrow pilot scope to quantify admin time + waste savings first, then decide based on data.

**"Not a priority"**
Makes sense. When would this become urgent — office expansion, cost review, or employee experience goals?

**"We already have a process"**
Great. Often the win is not replacing everything, but improving visibility and reducing coordination friction around your current setup.

---

## 8) CTA options
- "Open to a 15-minute benchmark call next week?"
- "Want a 1-page lunch ops assessment checklist?"
- "Should I send two rollout options for teams your size?"

---

## 9) Usage rule
- Personalize first line + one context detail minimum.
- Don’t send unchanged templates.
- Track replies by message type (problem-led vs trigger-led) to improve copy quickly.
