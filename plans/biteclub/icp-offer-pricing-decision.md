# Bite Club – ICP + Offer/Pricing Decision Artifact (v1)

_Last updated: 2026-02-16_

## 1) Decision Summary (so you can move fast)

**Primary ICP to focus now:**
- **Segment:** Mid-sized companies in Germany with **150–1,500 employees** and office-based teams.
- **Buyer:** Workplace/Office/People Ops (owns employee experience + lunch operations).
- **Economic buyer:** CFO/Finance or COO (cost control, process efficiency).
- **Pain fit:** Lunch chaos, missing transparency, no usage analytics, manual vendor coordination, food waste.

**Core GTM wedge (first 90 days):**
- Sell as **"Betriebsrestaurant-light without operational overhead"** for offices with hybrid teams.

**Recommended pricing model:**
- Start with **Per Standort (site) + usage cap tiers** (not per-user, not pure per-order).
- Add optional setup fee for onboarding + canteen rollout.

Why: easiest procurement conversation, predictable invoice, clear value framing for ops/finance.

---

## 2) ICP Definition (v1)

## Ideal Customer Profile (Tier A)
- DACH office employers, 150–1,500 employees.
- 1–10 office sites.
- Existing lunch subsidy, canteen budget, or recurring catering spend.
- Decision urgency around: retention/culture, cost pressure, return-to-office experience.
- No fully mature internal digital food ops stack.

## Exclusion Criteria (avoid for now)
- <80 employees (often too price-sensitive / too low urgency).
- Pure manufacturing without office lunch ownership.
- Organizations requiring long public procurement cycles (>6 months) in first pass.

## Buyer Committee Map
1. **Champion:** Office/Workplace/People Ops Manager
2. **Approver:** Head of Ops / COO / CFO
3. **Influencer:** HR Lead (employer branding) + IT (basic compliance check)

## Trigger Events (best timing)
- New office opening / relocation
- Return-to-office policy updates
- Rising food/catering costs
- Employee engagement drop / employer branding push

## 3) Offer Structure (productized for sales)

## Core Promise
"Bite Club helps office teams run a modern lunch program with less admin, better employee experience, and measurable cost control."

## Offer Stack (what is sold)
1. **Lunch Ops Platform** (ordering + coordination + transparency)
2. **Admin Console** (usage, budgets, reports)
3. **Employee Experience Layer** (simple ordering flow)
4. **Rollout Support** (playbook + onboarding)

## Outcome Claims to test in sales calls
- Reduce lunch coordination/admin time by **30–60%**.
- Improve lunch participation by **15–30%**.
- Reduce waste / unclaimed meals by **10–25%**.

(Keep as hypothesis until validated with customer proof.)

## 4) Pricing Decision Framework

## Option A – Per User (monthly)
**Pros:** scales with headcount
**Cons:** friction in B2B procurement, hard with fluctuating attendance

## Option B – Per Order Fee
**Pros:** easy to understand usage linkage
**Cons:** procurement dislikes variable invoice; can punish adoption

## Option C – Per Site Tier (recommended)
**Pros:** budget clarity, simple buying decision, aligns to office operations
**Cons:** needs careful tier thresholds

## Recommendation: Option C (Per Site Tier)

### Draft Packages (v1)

## Starter (1 site)
- Up to 150 active lunch users/month
- Core platform + basic reporting
- Email support
- **Price anchor:** €399–€690 / site / month

## Growth (1–3 sites)
- Up to 600 active lunch users/month
- Advanced reporting + budget controls
- Priority support
- **Price anchor:** €890–€1,490 / month

## Enterprise (multi-site)
- 4+ sites or custom usage
- SSO/compliance options + custom rollout
- Dedicated success support
- **Price:** custom / annual contract

### One-time onboarding fee (recommended)
- €750–€2,500 depending on site count/process complexity.

---

## 5) Commercial Guardrails

- Offer monthly + annual (annual with 10–15% discount).
- Keep discounting policy strict (max 15% without explicit reason).
- For first 5 lighthouse customers: allow pilot incentive, but always with conversion condition.

---

## 6) Validation Plan (next 2 weeks)

- Run 10–15 discovery/sales calls with the above ICP.
- Test willingness-to-pay using tier ladder (Starter/Growth/Enterprise).
- Track objections by category: price, procurement, integration, urgency.
- Decision checkpoint after first 10 calls:
  - Keep per-site tiers if conversion signals > alternatives.
  - Adjust price anchors by objection density.
