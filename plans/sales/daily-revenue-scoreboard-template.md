## Definitions (keep consistent)
- **New Leads Added:** net new qualified leads entered that day.
- **Outreach Sent:** first-touch outbound messages sent (LI + email).
- **Replies:** any response received.
- **Positive Replies:** reply that indicates interest/next step.
- **Calls Booked:** new meetings scheduled.
- **Calls Held:** meetings actually completed.
- **Proposals Sent:** commercial offer/quote sent.
- **Proposals Won:** signed/accepted proposals.
- **Revenue Closed (€):** contracted value closed that day.
- **Pipeline Created (€):** value of new qualified opportunities created that day.
- **Pipeline Total (€):** running total open pipeline value.
- **Win Rate (%):** proposals won / proposals sent (rolling period).

## Daily target suggestions (starting baseline)
- New leads added: **15+**
- Outreach sent: **25–40**
- Positive replies: **3+**
- Calls booked: **1–3**
- Proposals sent: **1+ per 2 days**

Adjust after 2 weeks with real conversion data.
