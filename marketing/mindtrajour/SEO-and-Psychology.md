# Trading Behavior & SEO Framework: MindTrajour

## 1. Trading Behavior (Research)
### Overconfidence Among Option Traders
- **Chen & Sabherwal (2019):** Positive relationship between turnover and past market returns (behavioral bias, not information arbitrage).
- **J. Risk Financial Management (2023):** Higher activity influences volatility spreads. Traders chase stocks with strong recent performance.
- **Pallavi G. P. (2026):** Positive performance feedback leads to significant rise in trading volumes (behavioral reinforcement).
- **Zhang & Chatterjee (2025):** Subjective overconfidence increases the likelihood of engaging in options trading irrespective of performance.

| Behavioral Pattern | Empirical Source |
| :--- | :--- |
| Strong trader overconfidence influences activity | Chen & Sabherwal (2019) |
| Heavy trading linked to perceptual biases | Chen (2023) |
| Rise in trading after past gains (bias) | Pallavi G. P. (2026) |
| Overconfidence increases options engagement | Zhang & Chatterjee (2025) |

## 2. Practical Marketing & SEO Framework
### Core Positioning Statement
Options traders respond more favorably to copy emphasizing **process, control, and discipline**—not fear or profit promises.

### Target Audience (Demographics)
- **Age:** 35–55 (Peak intensity in France: 45–64; US Mean: 47).
- **Gender:** Predominantly male (but non-trivial female participation).
- **Education:** Disproportionately college-educated (but still prone to discipline gaps).
- **Experience:** Majority < 3 years experience; high frequency (daily/weekly).

### Psychological Framework
- **Fear:** Use as a transition, not a destination. "Most traders lose money (Filter) -> Here is how you fix repeatable mistakes (Agency)."
- **Agency:** "You are in control" > "We make you money."
- **Language:** Use action verbs (*enter, exit, close, risk, max loss, breakeven, IV, gamma*) over marketing adjectives.

## 3. Messaging Hooks (Evidence-Based)
1.  **Self-monitoring:** "You can't fix what you don't measure."
2.  **Loss Aversion (Mistake Prevention):** "Stop paying tuition to the market for the same lesson."
3.  **Identity Reinforcement:** "Serious traders review their trades. Professionals track decisions."
4.  **Process Certainty:** "Consistency beats intensity. Same rules, every trade."
5.  **Cognitive Offloading:** "Your journal doesn't forget. Let the system remember so you don't rationalize."

## 4. SEO & Structure Strategy
### Intent Mapping
- **Transactional (Landing Page):** "options trading journal", "track options trades".
- **Feature Depth (Subpages):** "options risk management", "position sizing".
- **Diagnostic (Blog):** "why I lose money trading options", "common options mistakes".

### Technical SEO Requirements
- **H1:** "Options Trading Journal" (Exact). One per page.
- **YMYL Compliance:** No profit guarantees. Neutral, process-first language.
- **Taxonomy:** 
    - `/options-trading-journal`
    - `/features/trade-tracking`
    - `/blog/`

### Landing Page Narrative Order
1.  **Problem (Neutral):** Lack of review leads to repeating mistakes.
2.  **System Overview:** How logging + stats + emotion tracking work together.
3.  **Process Explanation:** Consistent tracking of entries/exits/risk.
4.  **Agency:** See patterns, fix mistakes.
5.  **Product Boundaries:** Not signals, not predictions.
6.  **Trust:** Clear distinction from generic spreadsheets.
7.  **CTA:** Start tracking.
