### The Silent Account Killer
Most options traders don't lose money because they lack a "matching strategy." They lose money because they have a "memory problem."

When you close a winning Iron Condor, your brain takes the credit. You feel like a genius. When a Bull Put Spread goes against you and you revenge-trade your way into a blown account, your brain performs a fascinating trick: **It deletes the memory.**

Psychologists call this *self-attribution bias*. In the trading world, we call it the "Silent Account Killer."

### The Science of Overconfidence
Research by **Chen & Sabherwal (2019)** into behavioral patterns of option traders highlights a dangerous cycle: positive market feedback leads to a significant rise in trading volumes—not because the opportunities are better, but because the traders become overconfident.

Without a ruthless, written record, you are flying blind. You are repeating the same emotional mistakes (FOMO, revenge trading, moving stops) and paying the market "tuition" for lessons you should have learned months ago.

### Why Your Spreadsheet is Failing You
If you use a messy Excel sheet, you aren't journaling. You are maintaining a graveyard for data. 
- **Friction:** If it takes more than 60 seconds to log a multi-leg spread, you won't do it during a losing streak.
- **Context:** Numbers tell you the "what," but they never tell you the "why." (Were you tired? Stressed? Revenge-trading?).
- **Avoidance:** It's easy to "forget" to open Excel when the day was red.

### The Solution: Radical Transparency
A professional **options trading journal** like MindTrajour doesn't just track your Greeks; it tracks your psyche. 

1. **The Mirror Effect:** It forces you to look at the trades your ego wants to forget.
2. **Pattern Recognition:** See exactly which emotions correlate with your biggest drawdowns.
3. **Discipline as a Muscle:** By logging every trade—especially the painful ones—you build the "Manager's Calm" needed to survive the market.

**Stop guessing. Start tracking. Your future self (and your P&L) will thank you.**
