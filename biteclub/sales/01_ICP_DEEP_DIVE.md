# Bite Club – Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) & Persona Deep Dive

## Target Company Profile
- **Size:** 100–1,000 employees on-site (Sweet spot: 150–400).
- **Industry:** Manufacturing, Logistics, Mid-sized Tech/Agency (with kitchen), Business Parks.
- **Location:** DACH region (Germany focus first).
- **Current Setup:** Has an on-site canteen OR orders catering daily. Uses paper vouchers, Excel lists, or "first come, first served" (chaos).
- **Trigger Event:** New office opening, recent growth (chaos increased), sustainability initiative (food waste reduction).

## Persona 1: The "Overwhelmed" Office Manager / HR
**Role:** Office Manager, HR Generalist, Happiness Manager.
**Goal:** Happy employees, smooth lunch operations, less manual work.
**Pain Points:**
- "Every day at 11:00 AM, Slack/Teams explodes with 'What's for lunch?'"
- Manually collecting orders in Excel or paper lists.
- Handling cash or subsidies manually (Excel chaos at month-end).
- Dealing with complaints when food runs out.
**Pitch Angle:** "Automate the lunch chaos. Save 5 hours/week on admin. Happy team."

## Persona 2: The "Cost-Conscious" Facility / Operations Manager
**Role:** Facility Manager, COO, Site Lead.
**Goal:** Cost efficiency, waste reduction, vendor management.
**Pain Points:**
- Paying for food that gets thrown away (Waste > 15%).
- Unpredictable costs due to fluctuating attendance.
- Subsidies are hard to track/audit.
- Kitchen vendor complains about planning uncertainty.
**Pitch Angle:** "Cut food waste by 30%. Pay only for what's ordered. Full transparency."

## Persona 3: The Canteen Vendor / Caterer (Partner Channel)
**Role:** Catering Company Owner, Kitchen Manager.
**Goal:** Planable production, guaranteed revenue, less waste.
**Pain Points:**
- Cooking based on guesswork ("Maybe 100 people come today?").
- Leftovers = Lost margin.
- administrative burden of billing the company.
**Pitch Angle:** "Get your orders 1 week in advance. Cook exactly what's needed. We handle the billing/ordering interface."

## Anti-ICP (Do NOT Target)
- **Too Small:** <30 employees (Slack message is enough).
- **Fully Remote:** No physical canteen needed.
- **Enterprise Giants:** (>5,000 employees) -> Long sales cycles, complex SAP integrations needed (Focus on this later).
- **Public Sector:** Tenders/Ausschreibungen take too long right now.
