# Bite Club – Pricing Proposal (Draft v1)

**Goal:** Remove friction. Get them to say "Yes" to a Pilot.

## Option A: The "No-Brainer" Pilot (Recommended)
*Perfect for validating the value in 30 days.*

- **Setup Fee:** €490 (One-time) – Includes menu setup, onboarding, QR codes.
- **Monthly Fee:** €0 (First Month)
- **Commitment:** 30 Days. Cancel anytime.
- **Result:** After 30 days, we present a report on waste reduction + employee usage.

## Option B: Standard SaaS License (Post-Pilot)
*Simple, predictable flat fee for the company.*

- **Small Site (<100 users):** €149 / month
- **Medium Site (100–300 users):** €299 / month
- **Large Site (300+ users):** €499 / month
- **Includes:**
  - Weekly menu planning
  - Digital ordering system
  - Kitchen dashboard
  - Monthly usage reports
  - Email support

## Option C: "Per Order" Transaction Fee (Alternative)
*Lower risk for them, but harder to predict revenue for us.*

- **Base Fee:** €49 / month
- **Transaction Fee:** €0.50 per order (paid by company OR user)
- *Note:* Often harder to sell because finance hates variable costs. Stick to Option B if possible.

## Add-Ons (Revenue Boosters)
- **Subsidy Management Module:** +€49/mo (Export ready for payroll).
- **Guest Ordering (External events):** +€29/mo.
- **Custom Branding (Logo/Colors):** +€99 (One-time).

## Pitching the ROI
"If we save you just **5 meals per day** from the trash bin (at €6 cost each), that's **€600/month** saved. The software costs €299. You make €300 profit just by using it."
