# Bite Club – Lead Sourcing Guide (30-Minute Sprint)

**Goal:** Find 50 relevant contacts in 30 minutes.

## Method 1: Google Maps "Industrial Park" Sweep
1. Open Google Maps for a target region (e.g., "Tuttlingen", "Rottweil", "Villingen-Schwenningen").
2. Search for: `Industriegebiet` or `Gewerbepark`.
3. Zoom in on large building complexes.
4. Look for company names with >50 reviews (indicates traffic/size).
5. Check their website -> Career page -> "Benefits" -> Look for "Kantine" or "Essenszulage".
6. **If yes:** Add to list.

## Method 2: LinkedIn Sales Navigator (or Standard Search)
**Search Strings:**
- `("Office Manager" OR "Facility Manager" OR "HR Manager") AND ("Tuttlingen" OR "Rottweil" OR "Konstanz")`
- `("Geschäftsführer" OR "Inhaber") AND "Mittelstand" AND ("Produktion" OR "Maschinenbau")`

**Filter:**
- Company Headcount: 51–200, 201–500.
- Industry: Machinery, Manufacturing, Automotive, Logistics.

## Method 3: "Wer liefert was" (wlw.de)
1. Search for `Maschinenbau` in `78xxx` (PLZ).
2. Filter by Employees > 100.
3. Grab the generic `info@` or `karriere@` email if no direct contact found (use specific subject line to route it).

## Method 4: The "Canteen Hunter"
1. Search Google for: `site:de "unsere kantine" speiseplan`
2. Search for: `site:de "betriebsrestaurant" öffnungszeiten`
3. This finds companies that *definitely* have a canteen and publish menus online (often PDFs).
4. **Goldmine:** These are the hottest leads. They have a canteen, and they are likely using old-school PDF uploads.

## Qualification Checklist (Do they match?)
- [ ] Physical location with >50 people?
- [ ] Canteen or Catering service mentioned?
- [ ] Not a massive corporation (Siemens, Bosch)?
- [ ] Decision maker visible on LinkedIn/Xing?

## Data Entry (Excel/Notion)
Columns: `Company Name`, `Contact Person`, `Role`, `Email/LinkedIn`, `Canteen Confirmed?`, `Status` (New, Contacted, Replied).
