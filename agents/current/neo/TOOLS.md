## Formeln

### CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost)
= Total Sales & Marketing Spend / New Customers

### LTV (Lifetime Value)
= ARPU × Gross Margin % / Churn Rate

### Payback Period
= CAC / (ARPU × Gross Margin %)

### MoM Growth Rate
= (Current MRR - Previous MRR) / Previous MRR × 100

## Benchmarks SaaS B2B (DACH, SMB)
- Churn: <5%/Monat = gut
- NPS: >40 = gut
- CAC Payback: <12 Monate = gut
- LTV/CAC: >3x = gut
