# ai-stromlaufplan

**Type:** Desktop/Web App
**Domain:** Electrical Engineering / Trades
**Description:** AI-powered tool for electricians to create and work with Stromlaufpläne (electrical circuit diagrams / wiring schematics).

## Terms
- Stromlaufplan = electrical circuit diagram (German standard, DIN/VDE)
- KI = Künstliche Intelligenz = AI

## Notes
- Niche B2B tool targeting electricians / electrical engineers
- German market likely primary target
