## Overview

- **16 market/EN keywords** -- general category keywords across Germany, Nigeria, USA with volume + KD data
- **77 German language keywords** -- specific German-market keywords organized by type and intent

**German keyword types:**
- **Brand** -- branded searches for MindTrajour directly
- **Brand + Niche** -- branded + options/trading qualifier
- **Local/Niche** -- German-language niche terms
- **Niche** -- options trading niche terms (non-branded)
- **Broad/Gen** -- high-volume general trading terms

**Intent codes:**
- **Nav** -- Navigational (user looking for MindTrajour directly)
- **Info** -- Informational (learning / research)
- **Comm** -- Commercial (evaluating / comparing)
- **Trans** -- Transactional (ready to sign up / buy)

## Key Observations

1. **Brand keywords** -- MindTrajour branded terms are low-volume Nav/Trans intent. Good for brand SEO but not major traffic drivers.
2. **Niche core terms** -- "options trades tracken", "optionsstrategie journal", "multi-leg optionen journal" -- very low volume but high purchase intent. Ideal for feature pages and conversion.
3. **Broad/Gen terms** -- "optionshandel fuer anfaenger", "optionsstrategien", "trading lernen" -- high volume, high competition. Blog content strategy territory.
4. **Trans intent opportunities** -- "trading journal kostenlos testen", "trading tool testen" -- direct conversion opportunity. Good for pricing/landing pages.
5. **FAQ opportunities** -- "wie fuehrt man ein trading journal", "warum trading journal wichtig ist" -- FAQ / help center content.
